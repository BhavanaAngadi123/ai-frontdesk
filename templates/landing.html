— why it wins

Everything the big tools forgot.

Chat-only bots can't take bookings. Voice-only receptionists can't show your catalog. Team software drowns a solo owner. {{ app_name }} was designed around one person: you.

01

The owner brief

Every escalation arrives as a three-line brief — what happened, what the AI already tried, what you must decide. Claim it or resolve it in one tap, and reply inside the same chat. The customer never repeats themselves.

02

One box, every job

FAQs, catalog lookup, appointment slots, payment ↔ order matching — one AI, one thread. Every payment is matched to its order, the customer is confirmed instantly, and the order flips to confirmed only when the payment verifies. No tab-switching, no "please hold".

03

Trust by design

It introduces itself as AI. Discounts and refunds wait for your approval. Answers cite their source. And it never guesses when it's unsure — it brings you in instead.

04

Live in an afternoon

A 5-step setup wizard: business details, language, catalog, hours, WhatsApp. Done-for-you feel, without the agency invoice.

05

Speaks vernacular

English today, Hindi ready, Hinglish and more one translation file away. Your customers talk like themselves — so does your front desk.

06

Flat-friendly, single-tenant

One deployment per shop, clean data separation, SQLite to start and Postgres when you grow. No per-seat traps, no message markups baked into the design.

— how it works

From zero to answering customers in five steps.

1

Chat endpoint

Customers talk on your website or WhatsApp. One API, every channel.

2

Intent + tools

The engine figures out what they want and runs the right tool — FAQ, catalog, slots, payments.

3

Answer or escalate

Confident? It answers with sources. Sensitive or stuck? It writes you a brief and brings you in — same thread, no reset.

4

You decide in one tap

Claim, reply, resolve. Plain-language analytics tell you what you missed and what converted.

— for developers

Pluggable where it matters.

Bring your own LLM (any OpenAI-compatible API), your own payment webhook, your own Meta credentials. SQLite today, Postgres with one env var. Docker-ready.

git clone <repo> && cd ai-frontdesk
cp .env.example .env        # add LLM_API_KEY to leave demo mode
pip install -r requirements.txt
uvicorn app.main:app --reload
# → http://127.0.0.1:8000  (landing)
# → http://127.0.0.1:8000/dashboard  (owner side)
